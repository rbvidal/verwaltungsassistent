# Domain: Human Resources (Personalwesen)

## Purpose

Support HR departments with salary classification, leave entitlement calculations, working time regulations, and employment law compliance for public sector employees under TV-L/TVöD.

## Governing Regulations

| Regulation | Scope |
|-----------|-------|
| TV-L / TVöD | Tarifvertrag für den öffentlichen Dienst der Länder / des Bundes |
| TVÜ-L / TVÜ-Bund | Transition regulations |
| UrlVO | Urlaubsverordnung (leave regulation) |
| AZVO | Arbeitszeitverordnung (working time regulation) |
| TzBfG | Teilzeit- und Befristungsgesetz |
| BEEG | Bundeselterngeld- und Elternzeitgesetz |
| BPersVG | Personalvertretungsgesetz |
| AGG | Allgemeines Gleichbehandlungsgesetz |

## Required Structured Knowledge

### Salary Tables (TV-L)
- **Entgelttabelle 2025** — EG 1 through EG 15 with Stufen 1-6 (✅ partially implemented: EG 1, 5, 8, 9a, 9b, 9, 10, 11, 12-15 at Stufe 3 only)
- **Entgelttabelle 2024** — for increase calculations (✅ partially implemented)
- **Jahressonderzahlung** — annual special payment percentages
- **Zulagen** — allowances (Schichtzulage, Erschwerniszulage, Ballungsraumzulage)
- **TVöD tables** — federal employees (not yet implemented)

### Vacation Tables
- Erholungsurlaub: 30 days/year (standard), increased by age/service
- Sonderurlaub: marriage, relocation, family care
- Bildungsurlaub: 5 days/year in most states
- Vacation accrual during part-time, parental leave, illness

### Working Time Tables
- Standard: 39 hours/week (TV-L), 38.5 hours/week (some states)
- Gleitzeitrahmen: typically 06:00–20:00 with Kernarbeitszeit 09:00–15:00
- Maximum daily: 10 hours (ArbZG)
- Part-time options: 50%, 60%, 80% models

### Leave Types
- Elternzeit: up to 3 years per child
- Pflegezeit: up to 6 months
- Sonderurlaub: various grounds
- Sabbatical models
